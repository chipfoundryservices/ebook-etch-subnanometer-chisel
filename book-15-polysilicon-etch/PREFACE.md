# Preface: The Gate Etch Precision Challenge

Polysilicon etch is where semiconductor manufacturing meets the laws of physics and economics simultaneously.

A 3nm technology node requires gate lengths of 18-24nm (after multiple patterning steps). The polysilicon etch must achieve:

- **Gate CD variation:** ±1-2nm across 300mm wafer (±5-10% uniformity)
- **Line roughness:** <2nm RMS (prevents leakage current)
- **Oxide damage:** <0.5nm silicon consumption (preserves gate oxide integrity)
- **Equipment uptime:** >99% (gate etch is often critical path)

Fail at any one metric, and entire fab yield collapses.

This is why gate etch chambers command $10-15M capex and why process engineers specializing in gate etch earn $300k+ salaries.

This book reveals why polysilicon etch is simultaneously a physics problem, an engineering problem, and an economics problem.
