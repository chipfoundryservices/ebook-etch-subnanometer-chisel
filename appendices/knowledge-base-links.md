## C.2 Academic and Research Resources

### Plasma Physics Textbooks

**"Fundamentals of Plasma Physics"** by J. D. Callen (University of Wisconsin)
- Comprehensive treatment of plasma dynamics, kinetic theory, and wave phenomena
- Available through: University library systems, IEEE Xplore
- Covers: Debye shielding, plasma oscillations, particle collisions

**"Plasma Physics and Fusion Energy"** by J. D. Callen (MIT Press)
- Practical applications of plasma physics including etch and deposition
- Includes numerical examples and engineering applications

**"Low-Temperature Plasmas and Applications"** by K. H. Becker et al.
- Specific focus on non-thermal plasma used in semiconductor processing
- Covers: Ion sheath physics, plasma chemistry, diagnostic methods

---

### Semiconductor Processing References

**"Semiconductor Processing: Advanced Topics"** by S. A. Campbell (Oxford)
- Chapters on plasma etching, selectivity mechanisms, process windows
- Mathematical treatment of etch rate dependencies

**"Microelectronic Processing: An Introduction"** by A. S. Grove (Addison-Wesley, classic)
- Foundational text; still relevant for fundamental concepts
- Covers: Wet etching (historical), early RIE, selectivity basics

**"VLSI Technology"** (edited by Brews and Kamins)
- Standard reference for integrated circuit manufacturing
- Section 5 (Etching) covers RIE, ICP, selectivity
- Discusses 3D NAND architecture and advanced challenges

---

### Professional Journals and Conferences

## C.11 How to Access Research and Specifications

### University Library Access

Most research institutions have access to:
- IEEE Xplore (all IEEE journals)
- Wiley Online Library (JVSTA, semiconductor journals)
- ScienceDirect (Elsevier journals including JVST)
- Web of Science (literature search and citation tracking)

**Recommendation:** Use your university's proxy or VPN to access paywalled journals.

### Open-Access Resources

- **arXiv (https://arxiv.org/)** — Preprints of physics and materials science papers
- **ResearchGate (https://www.researchgate.net/)** — Researchers often share papers
- **Google Scholar (https://scholar.google.com/)** — Many papers have free PDF links

### Equipment Datasheets

- Most equipment datasheets require NDA or authorized access
- Process engineering students can request through their fab contacts
- Some datasheets are published in conference proceedings (IEDM, VLSI, AVS)
